# Easy Stems — marketing kit

Everything in this folder was produced from the shipping app (v0.1.7).
The full brief, with positioning, comparison table, website copy, social
posts, launch email, the video script and the FAQ, lives in the shared
document "Easy Stems — Marketing Brief"; this file is the short form.

## One line

**Any song. Every instrument. Your computer.**

Easy Stems splits any song into its instruments on your own computer, in
one click, with nothing uploaded anywhere: drums, bass, guitar, keys, lead
vocals, backing vocals and everything else, each as its own track you can
mute, solo, level and export.

## Three proof points

- Six instruments, not four. Guitar and keys come out on their own.
- Lead vocal and backing vocals, apart. Keep the harmonies, lose the singer.
- Runs on your machine. Unreleased material never leaves the room.
